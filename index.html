How it works
      
Every 0.2 simulated seconds the browser turns telemetry plus a guidance hint into one sentence and POSTs it to your local Ollama at /v1/systemone with two choice questions: rotation (left / hold / right) and engine (off / half / full). The model answers with a probability per option and no generated text. The chosen options become the next command, plain JavaScript integrates the physics, and the next question goes out while this stage animates.

      
Physics and guidance are a line-for-line port of lunar-laya. Nothing leaves this machine: no CDN, no cloud, no build step.
